# W-000: [EXAMPLE] Applying from a phone is painful / スマホからの応募がつらい

- **Submitted by / 提案者:** (example entry / 記入例)
- **Date / 日付:** 2026-06-11

## Who is stuck? / 誰が困っている?
International students — they browse jobs on their phone between classes, but our workspace is desktop-first.
留学生。授業の合間にスマホで求人を見ているのに、ワークスペースがPC前提。

## What is the problem? / 何に困っている?
They find a job on their phone, but have to wait until they're at a laptop to prepare the application. By then the motivation (and sometimes the job posting) is gone.
スマホで求人を見つけても、応募準備はPCに着くまでお預け。その間にやる気も(時には求人も)消える。

## Solution idea (optional) / 解決アイデア(任意)
Maybe a "save to workspace" mobile flow, or a mobile-friendly apply-prep screen. Not sure which — that's for prioritization to decide.
「あとでワークスペースで開く」導線か、モバイル対応の応募準備画面か。形は優先順位付けの場で。

## Impact guess / インパクト予想: L
Most of our target users are mobile-first.

## Why now? (optional) / 急ぐ理由(任意)
Semester break hiring season starts soon — peak application period.
